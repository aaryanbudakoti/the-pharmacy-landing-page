Frequently asked questions

Do I need a prescription for all medicines?

Schedule H and prescription drugs require a valid doctor’s prescription. OTC medicines, healthcare and wellness products can be purchased without one.

Do you deliver medicines at home?

Yes. We offer fast home delivery across Old Rajinder Nagar and nearby Delhi areas. Just message us on WhatsApp at 08735898101.

What are your business hours?

We are open every day from 7:30 AM to 10:00 PM, including Sundays and most public holidays.

Do you stock healthcare and wellness products?

Absolutely — vitamins, supplements, baby care, mother & child, surgical, ayurvedic and personal care items are all available.

Can you support emergency medicine needs near Ganga Ram Hospital?

Yes. We are right opposite the hospital and prioritise urgent prescriptions for patients and attendants.
